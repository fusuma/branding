## Section 5: Process (How I Work)

**Layout notes:** White background. Horizontal timeline or numbered vertical steps on desktop. Each step has a number (in amber), a heading, and a short description. Clean, structured, easy to scan. On mobile: stacked vertically.

---

**Section header:**
How I Work

**Section subheader:**
A clear, proven process that keeps projects on track and outcomes high.

---

**Step 1: Discovery Call**
We start with a free 30-minute conversation. I learn about your product, your team, your timeline, and what success looks like. I ask the questions most engineers skip.

**Step 2: Proposal and Alignment**
Within a few days, I share a clear proposal: scope, approach, timeline, and investment. No surprises, no hidden fees. We align on expectations before any work begins.

**Step 3: Architecture and Setup**
Before writing production code, I define the technical approach -- component structure, state management, design token integration, performance budgets. You review and approve the foundation.

**Step 4: Build and Iterate**
I work in focused sprints with regular check-ins. You see real progress every few days, not just at the end. I ship incrementally, gather feedback early, and adjust course as needed.

**Step 5: Deliver and Support**
The final delivery includes clean, documented code, a thorough handoff, and a post-launch support window. Your team can maintain and extend what I built without needing me -- though many clients choose to keep working together.

## Section 7: FAQ

**Layout notes:** White background. Accordion-style layout -- each question is visible, click to expand the answer. Deep blue text for questions, regular body text for answers. Amber accent on the expand/collapse icon.

---

**Section header:**
Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: What types of projects do you take on?**

I specialize in frontend architecture, design system development, and design-informed product engineering. Most of my projects involve React and TypeScript, though I adapt to the tools your team uses. I work on both greenfield builds and modernization of existing codebases.

---

**Q: How do you charge?**

I offer project-based pricing for well-defined scopes and weekly/monthly retainers for ongoing partnerships. Every engagement starts with a clear proposal so there are no surprises. I'm happy to discuss pricing on our initial call.

---

**Q: How long does a typical project take?**

It depends on scope, but most projects range from 4 to 16 weeks. I'll give you a realistic timeline during our discovery call, and I build in buffer for iteration. I don't promise fast if it means cutting corners.

---

**Q: Do you work with agencies?**

Yes, agencies are one of my core client types. I'm experienced in white-label arrangements, working within agency timelines and tools, and collaborating directly with agency design teams. Your client sees your brand; I make sure the engineering matches your standards.

---

**Q: Can you work with our existing team?**

Absolutely. Many of my engagements involve embedding with an existing product or engineering team. I adapt to your workflow, tools, and communication style. I'm comfortable with Slack, Linear, Jira, GitHub, Figma -- whatever your team uses.

---

**Q: What does your availability look like?**

I take on a limited number of projects at a time to ensure every client gets my full attention. Current availability is listed on my contact page, and I typically book 4-6 weeks in advance. If you have a project in mind, I recommend reaching out early.

---

**Q: What if the project scope changes?**

It happens, and I plan for it. I build flexibility into every proposal. If the scope shifts significantly, we'll revisit the timeline and investment together -- transparently and without friction.
