## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### LinkedIn

**Audience:** CTOs, VP Engineering, product leads, agency founders, hiring managers.
**Purpose:** Thought leadership, portfolio visibility, professional credibility.
**Tone:** Professional but not corporate. Insightful, reflective, peer-to-peer.
**Post frequency:** 3-4 posts per week.
**Optimal posting times:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8:00-10:00 AM EST.
**Format preferences:**
- Text-only posts (highest organic reach)
- Carousel documents (for frameworks, step-by-step insights)
- Single image with portfolio screenshots
- Occasional video (under 90 seconds)
**Post length:** 150-300 words for thought leadership; 50-100 words for project shares.
**Engagement rules:** Reply to every comment within 4 hours. Comment on 5-10 posts from target audience daily. Never hard-sell in comments.

### Twitter / X

**Audience:** Developers, designers, tech leads, product people, indie hackers.
**Purpose:** Community presence, quick insights, driving awareness.
**Tone:** Sharp, concise, opinionated. Slightly more informal than LinkedIn.
**Post frequency:** 5-7 tweets per week (including replies and quote tweets).
**Optimal posting times:** Weekdays, 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM EST and 5:00 - 7:00 PM EST.
**Format preferences:**
- Single tweets (punchy takes, one-liners)
- Short threads (3-5 tweets for deeper topics)
- Code screenshots
- Before/after UI comparisons
**Character strategy:** Front-load the hook in the first line. Use line breaks for readability.
**Engagement rules:** Quote-tweet with added insight rather than plain retweet. Engage in relevant conversations. Be generous with credit.

### GitHub

**Audience:** Developers, potential collaborators, technical evaluators (CTOs doing due diligence).
**Purpose:** Technical credibility, open-source presence, portfolio proof.
**Tone:** Technical, precise, helpful, well-documented.
**Activity frequency:** Regular commits (aim for visible activity at least 3-4 days per week).
**Focus areas:**
- Maintain a polished profile README
- Well-documented repositories with clear READMEs
- Contribute to relevant open-source projects
- Star and fork strategically to signal interests
**Profile README should include:** One-liner bio, link to website, current focus, featured repositories, contact link.

## 20 Post Templates by Category

### Thought Leadership (5 templates)

**Template 1: The Counterintuitive Insight**
"Most people think [common assumption]. But after [experience/number of projects], I've learned that [counterintuitive truth]. Here's why: [2-3 supporting points]. The takeaway: [actionable conclusion]."

**Template 2: The Framework**
"My 3-step framework for [specific challenge]:
1. [Step + brief explanation]
2. [Step + brief explanation]
3. [Step + brief explanation]
What's your approach? I'd genuinely like to know."

**Template 3: The Before/After**
"[Time period] ago, I used to [old approach]. Now I [new approach]. The difference: [specific outcome]. What changed my mind: [brief explanation]."

**Template 4: The Unpopular Opinion**
"Unpopular opinion: [strong statement about code/design intersection]. I know this is controversial, but here's what I've seen: [evidence]. [Invitation to discuss]."

**Template 5: The Observation**
"I've noticed a pattern in the best product teams I've worked with: [pattern]. They all [shared behavior]. And the result is always [outcome]. Here's how to apply this to your team: [advice]."

---

### Portfolio (5 templates)

**Template 6: The Case Study Teaser**
"Just published a new case study: [project name]. The challenge: [one sentence]. My approach: [one sentence]. The result: [specific metric]. Full breakdown: [link]"

**Template 7: The Detail Spotlight**
"Here's a detail from a recent project that most people won't notice -- but users will feel. [Description of the detail]. It took [time] to get right. That's the difference between 'done' and 'crafted.' [Screenshot]"

**Template 8: The Metric Share**
"[Specific metric] improvement on a recent project. Here's what drove it: [brief explanation of the engineering decision]. Sometimes the biggest wins come from [insight]. [Link to case study]"

**Template 9: The Before/After Visual**
"Left: before. Right: after. Same feature, same data, same requirements. The difference? [Engineering approach]. Sometimes craft isn't about building more -- it's about building better. [Side-by-side image]"

**Template 10: The Process Reveal**
"Here's what the first week of a project looks like for me: [Day 1 activity]. [Day 2 activity]. [Day 3 activity]. No code until Day [X]. Because the best code starts with understanding, not typing. [Image of notes/architecture]"

**Template 12: The Mistake Confession**

## Content Repurposing Flow

```
Blog post (long-form)
  |
  +-- LinkedIn post (key insight, link to full post)
  +-- Twitter thread (3-5 tweets summarizing main points)
  +-- TikTok/Reels script (visual demonstration of the concept)
  +-- Email newsletter (curated version with personal commentary)
  +-- Carousel (framework or step-by-step extracted from the post)

Portfolio case study
  |
  +-- LinkedIn post (metric + one-paragraph story)
  +-- Twitter post (before/after image + one-line result)
  +-- TikTok video (screen recording walkthrough)
  +-- Email feature (in monthly newsletter)
```
