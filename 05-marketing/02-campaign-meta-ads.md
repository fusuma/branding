## Campaign Overview

**Brand:** Flavio Fusuma
**Website:** flaviofusuma.com
**Primary color:** #1E3A5F (deep blue) | **Accent:** #F59E0B (amber)

## Campaign 2: Portfolio Traffic

**Objective:** Traffic
**Optimization:** Landing page views
**Budget allocation:** 35% of Meta ad spend
**Duration:** Ongoing, creative refreshed biweekly

### Ad Set A -- Feed Ad (Facebook + Instagram)

**Primary text:**
This project started as a Figma file and a tight deadline. It shipped as a performant, accessible web app that the client's users actually enjoy using.

That's what happens when engineering and design work together from day one.

Browse the full portfolio and case studies:

**Headline:** Real Work, Real Results
**Description:** Case studies in design-informed software engineering.
**CTA button:** Learn More
**URL:** flaviofusuma.com/portfolio

**Visual direction:**
Carousel ad (3-5 cards). Each card shows a portfolio project: the left 30% of the card shows a cropped Figma design, and the right 70% shows the live product screenshot. A subtle arrow graphic connects them. Each card has a deep blue banner at the bottom with the project name in white and a one-line result ("40% faster load time" / "98 Lighthouse score" / "3x user engagement"). Final card is a solid deep blue with amber text: "See all projects -- flaviofusuma.com"

### Ad Set B -- Stories Ad (Facebook + Instagram)

**Text overlay sequence (3 story frames):**
Frame 1: "From brief to production" (white text, deep blue background)
Frame 2: [Portfolio screenshot with subtle animation -- a scroll effect] "Real projects. Real outcomes."
Frame 3: "See the full portfolio" (amber text) + Swipe Up CTA

**Visual direction:**
Three connected story frames. Frame 1 is purely typographic on deep blue. Frame 2 shows a device mockup (laptop or phone) with a portfolio piece, slowly scrolling to reveal the full page -- feels like a live demo. Frame 3 returns to deep blue with the amber CTA. Each frame holds for 5 seconds.

### Ad Set C -- Reels Ad (Instagram)

**Hook (first 3 seconds):** "Here's a project I built from zero to launch in 6 weeks." [Cut to screen recording of the live product]

**Script (30 seconds):**
[Screen recording scrolling through a portfolio piece]
Text overlay: "The brief: Build a fast, beautiful web app for [industry]."
[Cut to code editor: architecture diagram or component tree]
Text overlay: "The approach: Component-driven architecture. Design tokens. Performance budget from day one."
[Cut to live product: smooth interactions, fast loads]
Text overlay: "The result: 98 Lighthouse score. 40% faster than the previous version."
[End card: "More projects at flaviofusuma.com" on deep blue]

**Visual direction:**
Screen recordings feel authentic, not overly polished. Mix of code editor, browser DevTools showing performance metrics, and the actual product in use. Pacing is steady -- not frantic, not slow. Music is minimal. Text overlays use brand fonts in white and amber.

### A/B Variations -- Campaign 2

I work with CTOs, product leads, and agencies who need more than code -- they need a technical partner who understands design intent and ships accordingly.

Currently booking projects for Q2 2026. Free 30-minute consultation, no strings attached.

**Headline:** Book a Free Consultation
**Description:** Discuss your project with a design-minded engineer.
**CTA button:** Book Now
**URL:** flaviofusuma.com/contact

**Visual direction:**
Clean, confident portrait-style image or stylized illustration of Flavio at a workspace -- laptop open, design sketches visible, clean desk. Deep blue background gradient. The image feels warm and professional, not corporate. An amber banner at the bottom reads "Free 30-min consultation" in bold. Below the image, the ad headline and CTA are prominent. 1080x1080.

### Ad Set B -- Stories Ad (Facebook + Instagram)

**Text overlay sequence (2 frames):**
Frame 1: "Your next project deserves an engineer who cares about design as much as code." (white on deep blue)

Minimalist, type-driven. Frame 1 holds the statement in large, clean serif or sans-serif typography centered on deep blue. Frame 2 adds a subtle pulsing glow on the amber CTA text to draw attention. No photos -- this is about the words.

### Ad Set C -- Lead Form Ad (Facebook)

**Form headline:** Let's Discuss Your Project
**Form description:** Book a free 30-minute consultation to explore how design-informed engineering can elevate your next project.

2. Email (pre-filled)

4. Role (dropdown: CTO / VP Engineering / Product Lead / Agency Owner / Other)
5. Brief project description (short text)

**Thank-you screen:**
Headline: "You're on the list."
Description: "I'll follow up within 24 hours to schedule our call. In the meantime, browse the portfolio at flaviofusuma.com/portfolio."

**Visual direction:**
The form card uses deep blue as the header background with a white form body. The submit button is amber (#F59E0B) with dark text. Clean, minimal fields. The header shows Flavio's name and the tagline "Craft at the intersection of code and design."

### Ad Set D -- Reels Ad (Instagram)

**Hook (first 3 seconds):** "Stop hiring engineers who don't understand design." [Direct to camera or bold text on screen]

**Script (20 seconds):**
Text overlay: "Here's the problem:"
"Your designer creates something beautiful."
"Your engineer builds something... functional."
"There's a gap."
[Beat]
"I close that gap."
[End card: "Free consultation -- link in bio" on deep blue with amber accent]

**Visual direction:**
Quick cuts, bold text animations. Each line appears as a full-screen text card with a fast transition. The "gap" is visualized by a split screen: left shows the design mockup, right shows a clunky implementation. When "I close that gap" appears, the two sides merge into a polished final product. End card is clean brand lockup.

## Audience Targeting Recommendations

### Core Audiences

**Audience 1: Tech Decision-Makers**
- Job titles: CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, Engineering Manager, Technical Director
- Industries: Technology, Software, SaaS, E-commerce
- Company size: 10-500 employees
- Age: 30-55

**Audience 2: Product Leaders**
- Job titles: VP Product, Head of Product, Product Manager, Product Director
- Industries: Technology, Software, SaaS, Digital Media
- Company size: 10-500 employees
- Age: 28-50

**Audience 3: Agency Decision-Makers**
- Job titles: Founder, Creative Director, Head of Digital, Managing Director
- Industries: Advertising, Design, Marketing, Digital Agency
- Company size: 5-200 employees
- Age: 28-50

### Lookalike Audiences
- 1% lookalike of website visitors (all pages)
- 1% lookalike of contact form submitters
- 1% lookalike of portfolio page visitors (high engagement)

### Retargeting Audiences
- Website visitors (last 30 days) -- exclude converters
- Portfolio page visitors (last 14 days) -- exclude converters
- Video viewers (50%+ completion, last 30 days)
- Lead form openers who didn't submit (last 7 days)

### Interest-Based Targeting (supplementary)
- Interests: Software development, Web development, UI/UX design, React, TypeScript, Figma, Product management
- Behaviors: Technology early adopters, Business decision-makers

### Exclusions
- Current clients (upload customer list)
- Job seekers (exclude "looking for job" behaviors)
- Students
- Competitors' employees (if identifiable)

## Budget and Scheduling Notes

| Campaign | Daily Budget (suggested) | Schedule | Optimization Window |
|----------|------------------------|----------|-------------------|
| Awareness | $15-25/day | Mon-Sun, all hours | 7-day click, 1-day view |
| Traffic | $20-35/day | Mon-Fri, 7am-9pm | 7-day click |
| Conversions | $30-50/day | Mon-Fri, 8am-7pm | 7-day click, 1-day view |

**Total suggested monthly budget:** $1,950 - $3,300

**Optimization cadence:**
- **Daily:** Monitor delivery and pacing
- **3 days:** Check frequency (keep under 3 per week for awareness, under 5 for retargeting)
- **Weekly:** Review A/B test performance, pause underperformers
- **Biweekly:** Refresh creative to avoid ad fatigue
- **Monthly:** Full performance review, audience refinement, budget reallocation
