## Audience Targeting

### Custom Audiences
- Users who have visited software development agency websites
- Users who have searched for "hire senior developer"
- Users who have visited competitor freelance platforms (Toptal, Gun.io)

### In-Market Audiences
- Software Development Services
- IT & Technical Services
- Web Design & Development Services

### Demographic Targeting
- Age: 28-55
- Household income: Top 30%
- Exclude: Students, entry-level job seekers

## Tracking & Measurement

### Conversion Actions
1. **Contact form submission** -- Primary conversion
2. **Consultation booking** -- Primary conversion
3. **Portfolio page view (3+ pages)** -- Micro conversion
4. **Case study download** -- Micro conversion
5. **Time on site > 2 minutes** -- Engagement signal

### KPIs by Campaign

| Campaign          | Primary KPI        | Target    | Secondary KPI       | Target    |
|-------------------|--------------------|-----------|---------------------|-----------|
| Brand Awareness   | Impressions / CTR  | 4%+ CTR   | Brand search lift    | 10%+      |
| Portfolio Traffic | Sessions / Engagement | 3+ pages | Time on site        | 2+ min    |
| Lead Generation   | Conversions / CPA  | <$50 CPA  | Conversion rate      | 5%+       |

### Optimization Cadence
- **Daily:** Monitor spend pacing, pause underperforming ads
- **Weekly:** Review search terms report, add negative keywords, adjust bids
- **Biweekly:** Evaluate A/B test winners, pause losers, introduce new variations
- **Monthly:** Full performance review, budget reallocation, strategy adjustments
